About Us

On February 21, 1952, students in Dhaka gave their lives defending the right to speak Bangla. Today, UNESCO recognizes that day as International Mother Language Day.

Ekush Sacramento keeps that spirit alive here in California. We organize cultural programs, Bangla classes for children, and community gatherings that connect generations.
